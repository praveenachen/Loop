Loop mascot assets

These PNGs are the active mascot images used throughout the Loop app. Keep the filenames stable unless the corresponding component references are updated.

Active files:

- `logo-goose.png` - primary goose brand mark
- `goose-driver.png` - rides experience artwork
- `goose-reader.png` - study groups artwork
- `goose-trophy.png` - marketplace artwork
- `goose-backpack.png` - dashboard/profile mascot artwork

The app is already wired to these paths.
