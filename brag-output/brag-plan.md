# Brag Plan: Loop

## What is this app?
Loop is a verified campus companion for University of Waterloo students: rides, marketplace, study groups and messages in one trusted network (mobile app, Expo/React Native, with a Next.js backend).

## The angle
App-store-listing energy for a campus app. Three colour-coded feature cards (rides = yellow, study = pink, marketplace = green), each fronted by the app's own goose mascot, all using the product's real copy. The thread through the video is "verified": every card ends on a trust beat.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
Goose logo settles, "Loop" wordmark, then the app's own line: "Marketplace, rides, and study plans in one verified campus network."

## Key moments (the middle)
- Rides card: "Need a ride?" / "Match with trusted drivers in minutes." with the driver goose.
- Study card: "Need to study?" / "Join focused groups for your course." with the reader goose.
- Marketplace card: "Need stuff?" / "Buy, sell, and request with confidence." with the trophy goose.
- Trust: "Every profile requires a verified @uwaterloo.ca identity before messaging or transactions." (the domain is product copy, not a personal address.)

## Outro / punchline
Goose logo, "Loop", "Verified campus network." CTA line: "Made for Waterloo students."

## User flow worth showing
Home screen: Overview -> three quick actions (rides / study / marketplace) -> Campus Trust. Recreate the Home screen's quick-action row as the centerpiece, then zoom into each card.

## Tone
- Preset: app-store
- Creative direction: clean store-listing feature cards, nothing dramatic
- Interpretation: title-case feature names with one supporting line, slide/wipe transitions of 0.35-0.45s, generous holds so every line reads.

## Format: landscape — 1920x1080
## Duration: 20s

## Visual identity (from the project, mobile/theme/colors.ts)
- Background: hsl(42, 42%, 98%) warm off-white; soft panel hsl(45, 30%, 94%)
- Accent: rides hsl(47, 92%, 66%), study hsl(335, 75%, 58%), marketplace hsl(151, 63%, 40%), brand hsl(160, 62%, 34%)
- Text: ink hsl(20, 18%, 16%), soft ink hsl(20, 10%, 42%)
- Display font: Fredoka (600)
- Body font: Nunito (500-800)
- Strongest visual element: the goose mascots in `mobile/assets/geese/` and the tinted action cards.

## Share copy (draft)
Loop: rides, study groups and a marketplace, all inside a verified @uwaterloo.ca network.

## Audio direction
- Role: warm bed with sparse professional accents
- Music: happy-beats-business-moves-vol-1 (120 BPM)
- Music treatment: start at 0s, low-to-moderate volume, fade out over the last 1.5s
- Music cue guidance: preset read. Strong cues at 17.02s, 17.52s, 18.02s; beat grid 0.5s apart. Lock the outro reveal to ~17.5s mapped onto the video's timeline only if the track is offset to start there, otherwise detect at composition time.
- Audio-reactive treatment: subtle; bass energy lightly lifts the card shadow/glow
- SFX posture: sparse, motion-matched (card slide sounds, one soft chime on the outro)
- Audio-coupled moments: three feature cards arriving one by one; outro logo
- Restraint rule: no risers, no loud hits, nothing over the text reads
